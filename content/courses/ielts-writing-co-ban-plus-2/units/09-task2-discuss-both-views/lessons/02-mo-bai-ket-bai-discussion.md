## General statement – dùng chung cho cả hai loại đề

Mẫu: **Recently, there has been an argument about whether + S V.**

✅ *Recently, there has been an argument about whether children should learn to cook at school.*

Vì General statement chỉ paraphrase đề, nó **không thay đổi** dù đề có hỏi ý kiến hay không.

> ⚠️ **Lỗi thường gặp:** Thiếu *about*.
> ❌ *…there has been an argument **whether** learning history is useful.*
> ✅ *…there has been an argument **about whether** learning history is useful.*

## Thesis statement

### Có ý kiến (*Discuss both views and give your opinion*)

Đặc điểm chung: có **"I"** hoặc **"my"** → người viết nói rõ mình theo phe nào.

- *Although some people argue that S V, I still believe that S V because S V.*
- *In my opinion, X is more + adj…* / *In my opinion, X is a more + adj + way to + V…*
- *I believe that + S V.* / *It is my belief that + S V.*

✅ *Although some people argue that cooking should be taught at home, I still believe that schools are a better place to learn this skill because lessons are more organised.*

### Không ý kiến (*Discuss both views*)

Viết theo kiểu **nước đôi**: dùng *While/Although* để cho thấy bạn nhìn nhận cả hai phía nhưng **không** nghiêng về bên nào.

- *Although/While X is beneficial in some ways, Y also has its own advantages.*
- *Although some people argue that S V, many others still believe that S V.*

✅ *Although learning to cook at home is beneficial in some ways, school cooking lessons also have their own advantages.*

> ⚠️ **Lỗi thường gặp:** *Although…, **but**…* – sau mệnh đề *although* **không** dùng *but*.
> ❌ *Although historical knowledge is boring in some ways, but it also has its own values.*
> ✅ *Although historical knowledge is boring in some ways, it also has its own values.*

## Bảng tổng hợp

| Vị trí | Có ý kiến | Không ý kiến |
|---|---|---|
| General statement | *Recently, there has been an argument about whether S V.* | *(giống bên trái)* |
| Thesis | *Although some people argue that S V, I still believe that S V because S V.* | *Although some people argue that S V, many others still believe that S V.* |
| Restatement | *In conclusion, although S V, I believe that S V.* | *In conclusion, while S V, S V.* (nêu lại cả hai phía) |
| Recommendation | *It is recommended that…* | *It is recommended that…* |

> 💡 **Mẹo:** Cách nhanh nhất để kiểm tra thesis đã đúng loại chưa: đề có *give your opinion* → thesis **phải có I/my**; đề chỉ *Discuss both views* → thesis **không có I/my**.

## Mở bài & kết bài mẫu (có ý kiến)

Đề: *Some people think an international car-free day is an effective way of reducing air pollution; others think there are better ways. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.*

> **Mở bài:** *Recently, there has been an argument about whether having a global day without cars is a practical solution to air pollution. Although some people argue that this day can make the air cleaner, I still believe that there are more efficient methods because one day without cars has only a short-term effect.*
>
> **Kết bài:** *In conclusion, although a car-free day may raise public awareness, I believe that long-term measures are more effective in reducing air pollution. It is recommended that governments invest more in public transport.*

### Tóm tắt

- General statement: *Recently, there has been an argument **about** whether S V.*
- Có ý kiến → thesis chứa **I/my**: *Although some people argue that…, I still believe that… because…*
- Không ý kiến → thesis nước đôi: *Although X is beneficial in some ways, Y also has its own advantages.*
- Không viết *Although…, but…*.
- Kết bài: Restatement + *It is recommended that…*
