## Hai góc nhìn trong thân bài

| Góc nhìn | Ý nghĩa | Khi nào dùng |
|---|---|---|
| **TPV** – Third-Person Viewpoint | Trình bày **ý kiến của nhóm người khác** (*people, they, their*) | Đoạn nói về quan điểm bạn **không** ủng hộ; hoặc **cả hai đoạn** nếu đề chỉ *Discuss both views* |
| **FPV** – First-Person Viewpoint | Trình bày **ý kiến của chính bạn** (*I would argue…*) | Đoạn nói về quan điểm bạn ủng hộ (đề có *give your opinion*) |

> 💡 **Mẹo:** Đề chỉ *Discuss both views* (không hỏi ý kiến) → dùng **TPV cho cả hai đoạn**. Đề có *give your opinion* → đoạn 1 dùng TPV, đoạn 2 (phe bạn chọn) dùng FPV.

## Ngôn ngữ TPV

| Vị trí | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|
| **Topic sentence** | *On the one hand, there are some reasons why people think + S V.* |
| | *On the other hand, many people would argue that + S V.* |
| **Supporting idea** | *Many/Some people argue that + S V.* |
| | *Their first/second reason is that + S V.* |
| | *Another common reason/belief is that + S V.* |
| | *People also hold a belief that + S V.* |
| **Supporting detail** | *They believe that + S V.* |
| | *It is their argument that + S V.* |
| | *They think this is because + S V.* |

## Ngôn ngữ FPV

- *On the other hand, I would argue that + S V.*
- Supporting idea/detail sau đó có thể dùng các cụm dẫn thông thường: *Firstly…, This is because…, For example…*

## Lỗi thường gặp

> ⚠️ **Lỗi thường gặp:**
> - *some **reason** why* → *some **reasons** why* (sau *some* là danh từ số nhiều).
> - *Many people argue that the knowledge of history **not relevant**…* → thiếu động từ: *…history **is** not relevant…* (sau *that* phải có S + V).
> - *It is **them** argument that…* → *It is **their** argument that…*
> - *Another common **believe** is that…* → *belief* (sau tính từ *common* cần danh từ).
> - *They think this is **that**…* → *They think this is **because**…*

## Đoạn mẫu – TPV (Adapted FSF)

Đề: *Some people think young people should be leaders, while others think older people are better. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.*

> **[Topic sentence]** *On the one hand, there are some reasons why people think young people should be leaders.* **[Supporting idea 1]** *Many people argue that young people are more open to fresh ideas.* **[Supporting detail 1]** *They think this is because, with their energy and forward thinking, young leaders can create new ways of doing things.* **[Supporting idea 2]** *Another common belief is that younger managers are often more adaptable.* **[Supporting detail 2]** *It is their argument that these young people can quickly embrace new technologies and trends, which helps organisations keep up with a rapidly changing world.*

## Đoạn mẫu – FPV

> **[Topic sentence]** *On the other hand, I would argue that older people are better leaders.* **[Supporting idea 1]** *Firstly, older leaders usually have a lot of experience and knowledge.* **[Supporting detail 1]** *This is because they have worked in their field for a long time and faced various challenges, so they can predict future trends more accurately.* **[Supporting idea 2]** *Secondly, they often have a long-term vision for their organisation.* **[Supporting detail 2]** *For example, an experienced manager is less likely to make risky decisions only for short-term profit.*

> 💡 **Mẹo:** Nếu đề chỉ *Discuss both views*, đoạn 2 vẫn viết bằng TPV: *On the other hand, many people would argue that… Their first reason is that… It is their argument that…*

### Tóm tắt

- **TPV** = trình bày ý kiến người khác (*people, they, their*); **FPV** = ý kiến của bạn (*I would argue…*).
- Không hỏi ý kiến → TPV cả hai đoạn; có hỏi ý kiến → TPV đoạn 1, FPV đoạn 2.
- TPV: *there are some reasons why people think… / Many people argue that… / It is their argument that… / They think this is because…*
- Cẩn thận: *reasons*, *their*, *belief*, *because*, và S + V sau *that*.
