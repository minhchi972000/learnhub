## Mở bài: [Paraphrase đề] + câu giới thiệu theo biến thể

Câu đầu luôn là **paraphrase đề** (vd. *It is widely believed that…*). Câu thứ hai báo trước bài sẽ bàn gì – **khác nhau theo từng biến thể**.

| Biến thể | Câu thứ hai của mở bài |
|---|---|
| **Effects – Solutions** | *In this essay, some negative effects of X will be discussed before possible solutions will be proposed.* |
| **Causes – Effects** | *In this essay, the reasons why X happened and its effects will be discussed.* |
| **Causes – Solutions** | *In this essay, the reasons why X happened will be discussed before some possible solutions will be proposed.* |

## Kết bài theo từng biến thể

| Biến thể | Kết bài |
|---|---|
| **Effects – Solutions** | *In conclusion, the negative outcomes of X could include + N. In order to…, it is recommended that + [solution].* |
| **Causes – Effects** | *In conclusion, the main factor(s) that lead(s) to X is that S V. This can lead to + [effect]. In order to…, it is recommended that + [solution].* |
| **Causes – Solutions** | *In conclusion, the main factor(s) that contribute(s) to the problem can be + [cause]. In order to…, it is recommended that + [solution].* |

> 💡 **Mẹo:** Cả ba kết bài đều có thể khép lại bằng **In order to…, it is recommended that…** – vừa tóm tắt, vừa đưa ra đề xuất.

> ⚠️ **Lỗi thường gặp:** Dùng nhầm mẫu của biến thể khác. Ví dụ đề hỏi *causes and effects* mà mở bài viết *…before possible solutions will be proposed* → lệch yêu cầu đề, mất điểm Task Response.

### Ví dụ – đề "Childhood obesity" (Causes – Solutions)

> **Mở bài:** *It is widely believed that uncontrollable weight gain in children has become a major concern in many nations. In this essay, the reasons why childhood obesity happens will be discussed before some possible solutions will be proposed.*
>
> **Kết bài:** *In conclusion, the main factor that contributes to the problem can be poor eating habits. In order to limit the consumption of fast food, it is recommended that parents regularly prepare healthy meals at home for their children.*

## Cụm dẫn trong thân bài

| Đoạn | Topic sentence | Supporting idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Causes** | *There are some reasons why + S V.* | *One/Another possible reason is that + S V.* |
| **Effects** | *There are some consequences/problems of X.* | *X can lead to / result in + N/V-ing.* / *One/Another expected consequence of X is that + S V.* |
| **Solutions** | *There are several ways to address + N.* | *It is important for X to + V.* / *Firstly, X should…* |

Supporting detail: *To explain, … / To be more specific, … / This is because… / Doing so will…*

## Đoạn thân bài mẫu

Đề: *Childhood obesity is increasing in many countries. What are the causes? What can be done?*

### Body 1 – Causes

> **[Topic sentence]** *There are some reasons why levels of childhood obesity are rising.* **[Supporting idea 1]** *One possible reason is children's unhealthy eating habits.* **[Supporting detail 1]** *To explain, because of busy schedules, many parents allow their children to eat fast food, which is very high in fat and sugar.* **[Supporting idea 2]** *Another possible reason is that children may lack exercise.* **[Supporting detail 2]** *To be more specific, the time they spend on electronic devices has replaced the time for physical activities.*

### Body 2 – Solutions (khớp từng nguyên nhân)

> **[Topic sentence]** *There are several ways to address weight gain in children.* **[Supporting idea 1]** *Firstly, parents should prepare meals at home so that their kids can access healthier food options.* **[Supporting detail 1]** *Doing so will help children reduce their fat and sugar intake from fast food.* **[Supporting idea 2]** *Secondly, it is important for parents to encourage their children to take regular exercise.* **[Supporting detail 2]** *This is because physical activities can help children control their weight by burning excess calories.*

Kiểm tra checklist: **Who** = parents; **What** = prepare meals at home / encourage exercise; **How effective** = *so that…, Doing so will…, This is because…*; **Match** = giải pháp 1 ↔ ăn uống, giải pháp 2 ↔ thiếu vận động. ✅

### Tóm tắt

- Mở bài = paraphrase + câu giới thiệu đúng biến thể (*…will be discussed before… will be proposed*).
- Kết bài: *the negative outcomes of X could include… / the main factor that leads to… / the main factor that contributes to…* + *In order to…, it is recommended that…*
- Causes: *There are some reasons why… / One possible reason is that…*
- Effects: *X can lead to / result in… / One expected consequence of X is that…*
- Solutions: *There are several ways to address… / It is important for X to…*
